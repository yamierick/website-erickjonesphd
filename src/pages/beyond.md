**Writing.** I have written about engineering, policy, and ideas for over a decade at [Engineer Living](https://engineerliving.com) — essays and a running notebook on the energy transition, cities, and the systems that shape how people live. **Fiction.** I am writing a science fiction novel, *The Friction Deficit* — set in a post-scarcity solar civilization, it asks what happens to purpose, love, and meaning when survival costs nothing. The lab's vision statement ends "a blueprint for utopia here and among the stars"; the novel is about what we do when we get there.

**Travel.** All habitable continents with only a backpack — the origin story of everything above. The photos live [here](https://engineerliving.com/archive/pages/gallery/).
